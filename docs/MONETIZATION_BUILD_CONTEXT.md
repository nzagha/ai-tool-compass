## Non-negotiable business rules
- Organic rankings, review integrity, editorial decisions, aiBean Verified and Last Verified are independent of payment.
- Paid submissions/edits/verification buy a workflow, not editorial approval or a badge.
- Payment and Pro membership never grant Tool ownership, Admin or Creator capability. Independent claim review and one current owner per Tool remain required.
- Sponsored content is disclosed and separate from organic ordering.
- No fabricated actual revenue, commissions, payable balances or provider success.
- Real payment activation requires separately approved provider configuration, commercial authority and successful genuine provider tests.
- Ordinary User membership/browsing and profile/content reading remain free. Full pricing matrix requires login per supplied historical rule; teaser detail is unresolved.
